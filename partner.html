BudScout for Ohio retailers · Pilot exploration

Partner With BudScout

BudScout is building an Ohio dispensary discovery experience. We are exploring a small, opt-in pilot with retailers to learn what makes store discovery and product information genuinely useful.

Prelaunch notice: No retailer partnerships, live menu feeds, verified consumer analytics, or guaranteed results are represented here. The public product comparison is fictional demonstration data.

What we're exploring

Clear store profiles, links to retailer-approved menus, and easier directions for customers. Any future product offers would require retailer authorization, provenance and freshness checks.

What a pilot could involve

A conversation about your goals, confirmation of the right business contact, review of proposed store information, and an explicit decision about whether to participate. No menu integration or data publication without permission.

Retailer visibility and market intelligence

Explore how a future dashboard could summarize profile views, website-link clicks, direction requests and aggregated regional search interests. These are discovery events, not sales, confirmed visits or unique customers.

The interactive preview uses fictional sample counts and trends across 7-day, 30-day and year-to-date periods. Community feedback and verified profile management are concepts, not connected services. BudScout is intended to complement your existing POS, not replace it or require access to transactions or inventory.

What we'd ask from a pilot retailer

Feedback on the discovery experience; verification of public-facing store details; and, only if mutually agreed, an authorized way to receive product/menu information. Participation, pricing, timing and scope are not finalized.
